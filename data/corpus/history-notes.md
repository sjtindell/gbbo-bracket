## Regularities that survived a re-read

- This week’s package decides the boot more than last week’s Star Baker (Ahamed).
- Technical consistency shows up in finals more than in week-1 boots. Flashy showstoppers swing Star Baker.
- Handshake correlates with doing well, not with lifting the trophy. Never in technicals. Mostly signatures.
- Bread-week Star Baker tends to survive into the late season. Not a winner lock.
- Non-elim weeks are usually illness/fairness, then a catch-up double. Scoring rule should exist before it happens.
- Age cluster of winners is roughly 22–36 with a long right tail (Nancy 60). 67 is not in that picture.

## Judge-split

We almost never know. Do not code “Paul’s baker” vs “Prue’s baker” as a historical feature. If a recap in S17 explicitly says Nigella wanted one person gone and Paul another, write it in that week’s notes as an anecdote.
